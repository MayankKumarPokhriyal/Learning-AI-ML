# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

This course is for learners at every level — people writing their first line of Python and engineers preparing for senior interviews alike. We want participation in this project to be a welcoming, harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that helps:

- Being patient with beginners — every question is a fair question.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Focusing on what is best for learners and the community.
- Explaining *why* when you correct something, and crediting others' work.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments, in public or private.
- Belittling someone for their experience level or for asking questions.
- Sharing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Sexualised language or imagery, trolling, or personal and political attacks.
- Any other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews — and when someone is officially representing the project elsewhere.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and other contributions that don't align with them.

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer via a direct message on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/mayank-kumar-pokhriyal/). All reports will be reviewed promptly and handled with respect for the privacy of the person reporting. Consequences range from a private warning to a temporary or permanent ban from the project, depending on the severity and pattern of behaviour.
